# Changes

Newest first. Dates are when the build was pushed.

## Nov 13 2026

- **Apex DME Controls 0.1.0 (new app):** one GT3 page on the center screen: **engine map** 1 to 4 with your names (MHD or BM3; a confirm sheet first, only with the car stopped and the engine running), **exhaust valve** OEM / OPEN / CLOSED (BMW's own job works on a stock DME; MHD and BM3 use theirs), **Max Cooling** (MHD, engine running). No xDrive control, on purpose. No arm switch: a press sends. Open / Closed are held against the DME's 20 s timeout, Closed goes back to OEM above a set engine speed (default 4,000 rpm). Settings page behind the header's Settings tab: Tuning (Off / MHD / BM3, confirm), Maps (names), Units, Valve & cooling (valve at every start, hold, close limit), System (box colour, demo). Demo data sends nothing. Commands from cauliflowermossgrape's Engine Config (MIT; bm3_protocol.js and bm3_profiles.js copied from it). Prototype: `design/dme-controls-v1/`; tested in `tools/settings-preview/` on a stand-in DME, not yet on the car.
- **Apex Telemetry 0.6.1:** the header's **Settings** tab opens the new settings page (it still opened BMW's plain list, and the new page hid behind a separate Setup button at the bottom, which is gone). BMW's list stays one tap away: System tab, Every setting as a list. Kit: `ctx.ownSettings(fn)` lets an app take the Settings tab, `ctx.openSettingsList()` opens the list.
- **Apex Cluster 0.21.0, Apex HUD 0.15.0, Apex Telemetry 0.6.0: new settings pages** (design: `design/settings-v3/`, reviewed with Chase). GT3 look from a new shared `shared/gt3ui.js`: black, sections framed in the box colour with a header strip, Barlow Condensed caps; segmented buttons and − / + steppers instead of lists. The old Settings tab (which ran off the screen) is split into **Units** (US / Metric / Custom preset, one row per measure, live preview), **Shift & alerts** (redline, shift point, cold guard, test, warning limits with colour bars), **Badge & clock** (cluster), **Position & colours** (HUD, the Colours tab folded in) and **System** (box colour, display options, demo data, About with the engine's status). Cluster: Themes is now Layouts. HUD: the G-meter page is gone (its settings stay under All settings). New **Box colour** setting (White, Silver, Yellow, Orange, Red, M blue, Green) for the page and the cluster's / pit board's boxes. M Tricolour is gone (the kit falls back to Ice White). **Apex Telemetry** gets its own settings page behind a new **Setup** bar button: Readouts (its five small boxes can each show any of 17 values, settings tBox1..5; oil pressure, AFR and timing are read only while a box shows them), Units, Tyres (pressure limits now settings), Data (what the car answers for every engine value) and System. Browser harness running the real page code: `tools/settings-preview/`.
- **Apex Cluster 0.20.0, Apex HUD 0.14.0, Apex Telemetry 0.5.0:** a unit setting for each measure: **Speed and distance** (mph, mi / km/h, km), **Temperature** (°F / °C) and **Pressure** (psi / bar), each "Follow Units" by default, so psi with °C (or any mix) works. **Units (all)** still sets them together; torque and power follow it. Telemetry's boost numbers are a little smaller in bar (two decimals).
- **Apex Telemetry 0.4.2:** fuel rail, low fuel, load, gearbox and the intercooler read again. On the car only boost came through: the engine unit was asked for whichever value was due first, and boost (every 250 ms) was due again by the time each boost read finished, so nothing after it was ever asked. Now the most overdue value goes next, a value with no answer 3 times in a row is asked every 15 s, and a candidate that gets no reply gives up after 1.2 s instead of 4. A box without a value shows what the engine unit answered (e.g. F423 NRC 31).
- **Apex Telemetry 0.4.1:** boost control is white while fine and red when boost stays 3 psi off target for 0.75 s (no yellow, no green; a spool-up's lag doesn't count).
- **Apex Telemetry 0.4.0:** tyres show no colour while fine. Pressure against the car's target: blue 3 psi under, yellow 3 psi over, red 5 psi over. Temperature: yellow from 130 °F, red from 150 °F (settings Tyre warm / Tyre hot, reset once to these), nothing for cold (the Tyre cold setting is gone); the stripe and the wheel on the car light only when warm or hot.
- **Apex Cluster 0.19.3, Apex HUD 0.13.2, Apex Telemetry 0.3.1:** boost is intake manifold pressure. The cluster read the charge pipe (OBD PID 0x70, pressure before the throttle, which spiked to 27 psi on every lift and shift) whenever the sport display had no boost; it now asks the engine unit for manifold pressure first (DME 4A2D, then 580B, then OBD 0x87 / 0x0B) and uses the sport display only when that gives nothing. Telemetry's boost control compared manifold pressure with the charge pressure target (4AB0); its target is now the manifold pressure setpoint (57DB), and its actual the same manifold list.
- **Apex Telemetry 0.3.0:** new **intercooler** box (the B58 has no intercooler water temperature sensor): charge air before the throttle (DME 59D4) and after the intercooler (580F), the drop across it, and heat soak over outside, yellow / red when soaked. Gearbox, outside and range share the top row; engine load moves to the boost panel's bottom line. Blue for cold on every temperature (gearbox WARMING UP, outside ICE POSSIBLE, intercooler); tyre pressure numbers coloured by their deviation from target (green on it).
- **Apex Telemetry 0.2.0:** no more engine readouts the Apex Cluster dash already shows. New: boost control (actual, the engine's target and the deviation, a bar with the target marked, ON TARGET / UNDERBOOST / OVERBOOST), fuel rail and low pressure fuel, engine load against target, g-force split into lateral and longitudinal with peaks (fuel level is gone: the dash has it). Yellow = warning, red = near critical everywhere it means something: boost deviation, fuel pressures, tyre pressure (low against target; high only well over, as warm tyres gain pressure), tyre temperature, gearbox oil, ice, range. Engine unit identifiers from M Vitals.
- **Apex Telemetry 0.1.0 (chase0017-apps, new app):** a GT3 pit board on the center screen. Tyres (TPMS pressure against the car's target, temperature in cold / ok / warm / hot colours) round a car seen from above; coolant, oil, oil pressure, intake, AFR and timing; boost with its peak, gearbox oil, outside, fuel, range and g-force with peaks. Engine unit values over the kit's read-only link (Apex Cluster's identifiers, M Vitals' for timing and the gearbox). New kit font family `race` (Barlow Condensed Bold, `tools/bake_kit_font.py`), browser preview in `tools/telemetry-preview/`.

## Nov 11 2026

- **Cluster Themes 0.14.6, HUD Widgets 0.12.6:** the Time zone (clock) setting now also lists named zones (US Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska, Hawaii, Arizona, Canada Atlantic, Mexico, UK / Ireland / Portugal, Central Europe, Eastern Europe). HUD Widgets now makes way for Redline: while Redline holds the HUD, HUD Widgets draws nothing, and it comes back by itself when Redline lets go (it uses the kit's HUD claim, the same one Cluster functions uses).

- **Cluster Themes 0.14.4, HUD Widgets 0.12.4:** two new settings. Time zone (clock): the head unit's clock runs on UTC, so the clock could show 5 to 7 hours off; pick your offset from UTC (for example UTC-5 for New York in winter) and the clock shows your time. Automatic keeps the old behaviour. Fuel: the gauge now asks the engine unit for the fuel level in % (standard OBD), which is right on any car; where the engine unit doesn't answer, it works it out from the litres the car reports and a new Fuel tank size setting (65 L on an X3, 59 L on a 3 Series). On cars with drive modes the apps don't know yet (M cars' Efficient, Sport and Sport+), the Settings page now shows the value the car sends, so it can be added.
